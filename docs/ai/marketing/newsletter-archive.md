# Newsletter Archive

Every Chill-Dogs newsletter issue sent through Buttondown: the Buttondown link, the featured article, the converter pages linked, the charity spotlight, the closing call-out, and themes. Use it to plan the next issue without repeating a charity, a link set, or a call-out.

## Use this when

Planning, drafting, or scheduling a newsletter issue, picking the next charity spotlight, or logging an issue after it is sent.

Public archive: <https://buttondown.com/chill-dogs/archive/>. Add a row and an entry here within a day of sending each issue (newest first).

---

## Issue pattern

Each issue is about one article, with links to several related converters, one charity spotlight, and a closing call-out. Pinterest has been the call-out in every issue whose archive page is public since 2026-05-28; the format leaves room for others (site search, "forward this to a friend"). Issues 1–3 were shorter: one article plus two or three related links, with no charity and no call-out.

## Patterns and gaps to act on

- **Topic spread.** Calming, travel, and safety dominate. Cooling was the featured article only in issues 1–2 (April); cooling converters since then appear only as related links. Heat safety, comfort and orthopedic beds, and sleep have not been the featured article in any public issue.
- **Reused links.** `/calming/best-calming-products-for-anxious-dogs/` (issues 6, 8, 9), `/cooling/car-cooling-for-dogs/` (issues 1, 2, 11, 13), and `/cooling/dog-travel-hydration/` (issues 7, 10, 11) recur. Prefer a fresh converter in the next issue.
- **UTMs are inconsistent.** Issues 1–3, 6, and 7 carry none, issue 9 uses `utm_source=buttondown`, and issues 8, 10–13 use `utm_source=chill-dogs`. [`utm-rules.md`](utm-rules.md) lists `newsletter` as the example source. Pick one and use it going forward.
- **Dates.** Dates above are the ones shown on the Buttondown archive pages. Buttondown's own page metadata disagrees for some issues (for example issue 12 carries a July date), so treat the displayed date as the send date.
